# Problem Statement

## Problem
Students and young professionals often lose track of small daily expenses, so they overspend without noticing until month-end. Spreadsheets are tedious and most apps are heavy, online, or require accounts.

## Scope
A lightweight, offline, command-line application in Python that records expenses, enforces monthly budgets with early warnings, and produces spending analytics. Out of scope: bank integration, multi-user accounts, GUI.

## Target Users
College students, first-time earners, and anyone who wants a simple private expense log.

## High-Level Features
1. **Expense Management** - add, view, edit, delete, filter expenses (CRUD) with validation.
2. **Budget Control** - per-category and overall monthly limits with OK / WARNING (80%) / EXCEEDED alerts.
3. **Analytics & Reports** - category breakdown, top expenses, daily average, month-end forecast, CSV export.
